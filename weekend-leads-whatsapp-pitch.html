The Problem Today

Three things are slowing your team down right now.

Not lead volume, not tracking — the exact issues you've raised.

The 24-hour reply window

WhatsApp only allows free replies for 24 hours after the customer's last message. Once the team is offline over a weekend, that window closes before anyone gets back to them.

A slow, clunky interface

The current inbox is dense and takes too many clicks to find and reply to a conversation — it slows a 10-person team down instead of speeding them up.

Messages take too long to land

Even during business hours, notifications and incoming messages lag — so replies are slower than they should be, on a good day.
